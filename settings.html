Photo: it is turned into a doodle, so your original photo is never shown. Video: a short clip (up to 10 seconds, under 25 MB) that plays in a loop.

Reminders

Replies can use {name}, {count} (how many times you pressed Yes today) and {minutes}. In a reminder message, separate several messages with || and a random one is shown each time.
Each row: on/off, emoji, message, interval in minutes, character for this reminder.
